# Reborn 338

Reborn 338 is a clean-room reimplementation of Propellerhead's ReBirth RB-338,
the software groovebox Propellerhead released in 1997, whose version 2.0 has
two TB-303 bass lines, a TR-808 and a TR-909. It
plays ReBirth's own song files, and its 303, 808, 909 and effects were written
from measurements of how ReBirth sounds, not from ReBirth's code.

[![build](https://github.com/ljferdinand/reborn338/actions/workflows/build.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/ljferdinand/reborn338/actions/workflows/build.yml)

> **Active development has stopped.** This repository is shared, as it stood in
> October 2026, for anyone who wants to carry the work on. Issues and pull
> requests are welcome but may not be answered quickly. Start with
> [docs/HANDOVER.md](docs/HANDOVER.md).

## What is in it

- An audio engine in portable C++17 with no dependencies: two 303 slots, an
  808 and a 909, a distortion, delay, compressor and pattern-controlled filter
  (PCF) running as per-section chains, a mixer, a 16-step sequencer and song mode.
- Two 303 voices: Reborn's own, fitted to ReBirth by measurement and the
  default, and Robin Schmidt's Open303 as an alternative.
- Readers and writers for ReBirth's `.rbs` songs and Reborn's own `.rbsx`
  sessions, and a reader for ReBirth mods (`.rbm`).
- `reborn_render`, which renders any song to a WAV file with no audio device,
  and smaller tools for round-trips and mods.
- A JUCE app that plays, edits and saves songs, built as a Standalone program and
  as VST3 and CLAP plugins (AU on macOS), which can host a third-party 303 plugin
  in either 303 slot; and a separate Reborn Open303 instrument plugin.
- The measurement tools and the full research record behind every fitted
  constant.

## Quick start

On Windows, with Visual Studio 2022 and CMake:

```sh
cmake -S . -B build -G "Visual Studio 17 2022" -A x64
cmake --build build --config Release --parallel
ctest --test-dir build -C Release
./build/Release/reborn_render --seconds 8 --out demo.wav
./build/Release/reborn_render --song examples/acid_line.rbs --seconds 16 --out acid_line.wav
```

On Linux and macOS, configure with `-DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release` instead of the
Visual Studio generator, and the programs land in `build/` rather than
`build/Release/`. The first render plays a built-in demo; the second plays one of
the generated example songs in [examples/](examples/). Your own ReBirth songs
play the same way. To build the app and plugins, add `-DREBORN_BUILD_APP=ON` in a
separate build directory; CMake then fetches JUCE.

[docs/BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md) covers every platform and the app;
[docs/USAGE.md](docs/USAGE.md) covers the tools and the app.

## A clean-room reimplementation

Reborn's 303, 808, 909 and effects are its own implementations. Each was built by
generating a small song that exercises one control, having ReBirth export it,
rendering it in Reborn, and fitting Reborn's behaviour until the two measured
alike, one component at a time. The `.rbs` and `.rbm` formats were worked out by
inspecting files and by saving and packing content of our own choosing. The
research record in [docs/research/](docs/research/) keeps every measurement.

No ReBirth samples, songs, mods or artwork are in this repository, and none are
needed: by default the drum machines use Reborn's own synthesis, and the examples
are generated by Reborn's own code. Comparing against ReBirth, playing your
ReBirth songs and mods, or loading ReBirth-style drum samples needs your own
copy.

## Provenance and the limits of the clean-room claim

The claim is procedural, and this is what it rests on.

- Every value in the code comes from measuring ReBirth's audio output or its
  files as a black box. None comes from decompiled or disassembled code.
- A decompilation of ReBirth, produced with a third-party tool, exists outside
  this repository; [docs/PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md) says how it came about.
  From September 2026 it was consulted only to decide which measurements to run, behind
  a two-role procedure: one role measured ReBirth and wrote a behavioural
  specification with no recovered values in it, and a separate role, which never
  saw the decompilation, implemented and fitted the effect from that specification
  and the measurements. Nothing from the decompilation is in this repository.
- The mod reader undoes the scrambling ReBirth's ModPacker applies. Its key
  function was found by packing chosen content with ModPacker and reading the
  output, without disassembling ModPacker. It exists so that users can play mods
  they own.
- Some tables in the code are measurements of ReBirth's behaviour: the 55 PCF
  trigger patterns (`src/effects/pcf_presets.inc`), the drum level trims, and the
  distortion's output-level targets (`docs/dist_gain_target.csv`). They were
  measured from ReBirth's audio, not copied from its files.

Whether this is enough where you live is not something this repository can
settle; it records what was done so you can judge.
[docs/PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md) has the full account.

## Samples, mods and Open303

ReBirth's drum machines play recordings; Reborn synthesises its drums unless you
give it recordings, from a folder of WAV files with the names in
[docs/SAMPLES.md](docs/SAMPLES.md) or from a ReBirth mod
([docs/MODS.md](docs/MODS.md), which also says how far mod support got). The
Open303 voice, how to choose a voice and the Open303 plugin are in
[docs/VOICES.md](docs/VOICES.md).

## Plugins

Reborn runs as an instrument plugin in a DAW and can host a third-party 303
plugin in each 303 slot. As a plugin it follows the host's tempo and play/stop
and saves its whole session in the project (samples and mods by path), but it
ignores incoming MIDI, exposes
no automatable parameters and cannot host effect plugins.
[docs/PLUGINS.md](docs/PLUGINS.md) has the details.
