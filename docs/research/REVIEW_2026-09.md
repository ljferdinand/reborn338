clean-room rules, done on the new PC at the tip of `gui/stage1-docking`

## High

### W1. Saving a song drops the arrangement and all automation (measured)

`saveRbs` (`src/io/rbs_write.cpp:244`) never reads `Song::arrangement` or
`Song::automation`. Each instrument TRAK is written as a two-event tick-0
stub, pattern 0 and unmuted (`rbs_write.cpp:319-330`), and the mixer track
carries only a tick-0 dump plus an end marker (`rbs_write.cpp:303-317`).

Proved with a scratch harness that loads a shipped song, saves it through
`saveRbs`, and reloads it:

| Song | Arrangement rows (bars) | Automation events |
|---|---|---|
| Clodhopper | 43 (299) to **1 (120)** | 612 to **22** |
| Trakka Rekka | 55 (123) to **1 (124)** | 3315 to **22** |
| G-Spot | 179 (209) to **1 (8)** | 6022 to **22** |

This path serves three features, so all three lose data:
- "Save song" (`PluginProcessor::saveSongFile`).
- `.rbsx` sessions, whose RSNG chunk is `saveRbs` output (`src/io/rbsx.cpp:92`).
- The plugin's DAW state (`getStateInformation`, via `saveRbsxToMemory`).

A DAW project reopened with Reborn in it comes back with every device on
pattern 0. The arrangement editor built on 2026-09-18 has no way to persist
its work.

**Fix:** write each device's pattern-select timeline (param 1 at every bar
where the arrangement changes that device's pattern, expanded through
`repeats`), and write `Song::automation` back onto its TRAK by target, both in
tick order. **Verify:** extend `reborn_roundtrip` to compare arrangement and
automation and run it over all 16 demo songs. Loading is untouched, so this is
byte-neutral for renders.

**Fixed 2026-09-26 on `review/2026-09`** (9bdbf4a, 7f92ef2, 1cf3fbf, 13fc3d7).
The writer saves each device's pattern-select timeline and puts every
automation event back on its own track in its original order, with a
synthesised tick-0 dump only for the params the automation does not state.
The loader now also carries the DELY track (every event) and the COMP track's
timed rides as `AutoTarget::Delay` and `AutoTarget::Comp`, which the engine
ignores, so a save keeps them. (Since E3 the DELY track's tick-0 dump loads
into the start state instead, and its timed rides play.) The song length is
pinned by re-selecting bass 1's pattern at the last bar instead of restating
the channel volumes there, which had snapped a ridden fader back to its
opening value. GLOB is passed
through from the loaded file (`Song::globRaw`) with only tempo, loop length,
shuffle and skin patched. Result over the 16 demo songs: `reborn_roundtrip`
reports 16 round-tripped, 0 problems (Clodhopper keeps its 43 rows, 299 bars
and 612 events), and every re-saved song renders byte-identically to its
original, full rack and `--no-fx`, 45 s each. Pooled hash unchanged.

### W2. Saving re-encodes the faders linearly (measured)

The loader decodes the master, channel and send faders with the measured
exponential law (`faderGain`, `pattern.h:242`). The writer encodes them back
with `kb()`, which is `round(value * 127)` (`rbs_write.cpp:47`, used at
`:251`, `:261` and `:309`). The two only agree at the byte-100 anchor.

Round trip on the same songs:
- **Master:**
  - Nominal songs go from 0.00 dB to **+9.99 dB**, because gain 1.0 writes byte 127.
  - Clodhopper goes from -11.10 to **-24.05 dB**.
- **Channels:**
  - Clodhopper's 909 goes from -11.70 to -26.87 dB.
  - Trakka Rekka's three live channels all come back at +7.91 dB.
- **Delay:**
  - DELY byte 2, the straight/triplet grid, is never written, so every straight delay reloads as triplet.
  - The delay pan is hard-coded to 64 (`rbs_write.cpp:268-273`, `:338-340`).

**Fix:** add the inverse of `faderGain` for the master (unity 1.0) and the
channels (unity `kChannelUnityGain`), and write the DELY grid and pan from
`EffectInit`. **Verify:** the extended round-trip harness should show every
fader byte-exact.

**Fixed 2026-09-26** (9bdbf4a, 1cf3fbf). `faderByteFromGain` and
`panByteFromPan` are the exact inverses of the load laws, checked for every
byte at both unities (`test_fader_and_pan_inverses`); pan byte 0 comes back as
1, and both are hard left. The DELY chunk writes its grid (+2) and pan (+4),
the DIST track its unit on/off instead of a fixed 1, and the COMP chunk its
ratio and threshold instead of eight zeros. The stricter harness found one
more writer bug of the same kind: the drum accent row (column 0) is a value,
and it was written as 1 on both machines, so every re-saved 909 accent came
back at half depth (1263 accented 909 cells across 12 demo songs; 14 songs
had some accent-row difference once hitless cells are counted, not the 15
commit 1cf3fbf's message says). A loaded step now keeps its own value.

### W3. Save writes the song as loaded, not what the GUI changed

`saveSongFile` and `captureSession` serialise `engine_.song()`
(`PluginProcessor.cpp:152`, `:241`). The GUI does not edit the song's
mixer and effect records. It edits live engine state:
- `MixerChannel` (`mix_`) for level, pan, send and mute.
- The unit bypasses (`pcfOn_`, `distUnitOn_`) and the compressor target.
- The effect objects' own parameters, for the PCF, delay, distortion and compressor knobs.

None of that is copied back into `Song::mixerInit` or `Song::effectInit`
before writing. So a user who re-balances a song and saves gets the balance it
was loaded with. Pattern edits do survive, because the grids edit the song
directly.

**Fix:** a `captureLiveState(Engine&, Song&)` step before every save that
writes the live mixer, routing, unit switches and effect parameters back into
the song (inverse laws per W2). **Verify:** a test that edits via the engine,
saves, reloads, and compares.

**Fixed 2026-09-26** (80ec53a). `Engine::captureLivePanel` writes the live
mixer, routing, effect knobs and voice knobs into the song's PANEL
(`mixerPanel`, `effectPanel`, the knob headers), which the writer saves as
chunks. `saveSongFile` and `captureSession` (sessions and DAW state) both run
it; `test_live_panel_capture` edits through the engine, saves, reloads and
compares. It leaves the start state (the tracks' tick-0 dumps) and the
automation as loaded, which is how ReBirth separates the panel from the
recorded song. So a loaded song that is re-balanced and saved keeps the new
balance in its chunks, and still opens at its recorded start, except for the
master, which has no track and plays the chunk. (So did the delay until E3 was
fixed; it now starts from its track like the other effects.) A song built in
Reborn has no recorded start, and for one the writer
takes the tracks' dumps from the captured panel. `saveSongFile` also writes
the file after releasing the lock now (L2).

The capture as first written overwrote every chunk with whatever the engine
held, which after `play()` is the start state, so an unedited save rewrote
the panel. That is follow-up F1 below, fixed.

## Medium

### W4. A session or DAW restore played before its voices were in (measured)

Found by the adversarial verification of the save-path fix; it predates that
work. `restoreSession` (`app/src/PluginProcessor.cpp`) loaded the song, called
`engine_.play()`, and only then ran `applyInstrumentSlots`, which swaps in the
303 voices the session records. `play()` dispatches the tick-0 dump and fires
step 0, so both went to the voices about to be replaced: the new voices
started on the header knobs and missed step 0. Opening a `.rbsx` and every DAW
project reload take this path.

Measured with a scratch probe over the 16 demo songs, TPT voice, 90 s,
compared bitwise against a plain open (voices installed, `loadSong`,
`play()`): the old order (`loadSong`, `play()`, swap) differs on all 16, nine
of them within the first 10 s.

**Fixed 2026-09-26** (002f695, follow-up F2). The song and samples load under
the lock without `play()`, the voices are swapped with the lock released (the
swap helpers take it themselves, and `onVoicesWillChange` / `onVoicesChanged`
fire as before), and `play()` runs under the lock after them. In between the
engine holds the new song at the old one's position, so a `restoring_` flag
keeps `processBlock` silent until `play()`. The fixed order renders
identically to a plain open on all 16. Three low follow-ups to this fix (F6 to
F8 below) moved the flag's read under the lock, clear it on every exit, and run
`onVoicesChanged` after `play()` rather than before it.

### E1. The song end wraps to bar 1 where ReBirth goes silent (measured)

The loader sets the arrangement length to the last event on the instrument
and mixer tracks (`src/io/rbs.cpp:599-601`, `:737-763`). At the end the
sequencer wraps to row 0 and resets its tick counter and automation cursor
(`Engine::advanceStep`), so the instrument tracks' tick-0 dumps are
dispatched again. The mixer and effect start state is not: it lives in
`mixerInit` and `effectInit`, which `play()` puts back in Song mode since
2026-09-27 (R1 of that round, below), and the wrap deliberately does not, so a
second lap keeps the last rides. That is left alone on purpose. ReBirth does
not wrap at the song's end at all, so there is no second lap to match, and what
ReBirth's own Loop restores at its wrap is `PLAN_TRANSPORT_MODES.md` L2, for
Stage B to settle along with this finding.

Measured on Trakka Rekka against `renders/nofxexports`, same length
(214.1 s = 124 bars at 139 BPM):

| Bar | ReBirth | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| 123 | digital silence | -114.5 dB |
| 124 | digital silence | **-18.1 dB, which is bar 1 replayed** (bar 1 is -20.7) |

Seven demo songs have a stored loop length 1 to 3 bars past our arrangement
(A Taste of Haste 177/174, Fast Forward 250/249, Groove It Up 235/234,
Metallicon City 250/249, Trakka Rekka 124/123, Transfusion 155/154, KiloMix
547/545). Every whole-song render of those ends with the opening bars.

**Recommendation:** in song mode, hold the last row at the arrangement end
rather than wrapping, and do not reset `songTicks_` or `autoIdx_`. Pattern
mode and the GUI's own loop are separate cases (Stage 4's bar-loop).
**Measure first:** one more song whose final state is not silent, to confirm
ReBirth holds the last patterns rather than stopping. This changes renders past the
arrangement end, so it is a parity change for Luke's sign-off.

### E2. Effect automation past the last instrument event never plays

The same length rule ignores the DELY, DIST, PCF and COMP tracks. Measured
over the demo songs, DIST and PCF events past our arrangement end: A Taste of
Haste 29, Fonda Workout 38, SuperNova 12. They are loaded and never
dispatched. **Fix:** include tracks 5 to 8 in `lastTime`. This is a parity
change for those three songs' final bars.

### E3. The delay plays its chunk; ReBirth's Song mode plays the DELY track (measured)

The loader applies the `DELY` chunk (steps, grid, feedback, pan) to the delay
and carries the DELY track's events, tick 0 included, as `AutoTarget::Delay`,
which `applyAutoEvent` ignores. The two disagree in 8 of the 16 demo songs,
6 of which name no mod: Trakka Rekka's chunk has feedback 0 and pan 127 where
its track opens at 36 and 63. Code comments cite this finding in
`src/io/rbs.cpp`, `src/io/rbs_write.cpp`, `src/engine/engine.cpp` and
`include/reborn/pattern.h`.

Measured 2026-09-26 on four isolated fixtures, each one 909 rimshot through
the delay with the DELY chunk patched to disagree with the track's tick-0 dump
on steps, feedback and pan at once (`fixtures-2026-09-26/delay-chunk-vs-track`,
exports in `renders/delay-chunk-vs-track-2026-09-26`). In Song mode ReBirth
played the track's values in every case, to 0.1 dB. The copy naming the MSM2
mod loaded that mod's rimshot and still played the track's delay, so a mod
does not change which one plays. The chunk is the Pattern-mode panel and the
track is the Song (`PLAN_TRANSPORT_MODES.md` section 2).

**Approved** by Luke 2026-09-26. The fix, the delay playing its track in Song
mode, is task 2 of this run and comes after the follow-ups below.

**Fixed 2026-09-26** (57c7cfb). The loader reads the DELY track's tick-0 dump
into `effectInit` over the chunk values (steps, grid, feedback, pan, and a new
`EffectInit::delayOn` from param 0), consumed like the PCF's; the chunk stays in
`effectPanel`, and the writer synthesises the dump from `effectInit`. The timed
rides stay `AutoTarget::Delay`, and `applyAutoEvent` now plays them: param 0 sets
a new delay unit bypass, which skips the delay and its return, and params 1 to 4
set steps (clamped 1 to 16), grid, feedback and pan. The COMP rides stay unplayed.
Verified:
- Rendered before and after, our four fixture renders go from the chunk
  (dct_track one hard-right echo at 0.333 s, dct_mirror a centred train at
  0.667 s) to ReBirth's pattern on all four: the same echo times, centred or
  hard right as ReBirth is, and -2.4 dB per repeat against ReBirth's -2.4.
- Full-rack 90 s demo renders change in 12 songs: the 8 whose chunk and track
  disagree, and the 4 whose rides fall inside 90 s. The other 4 are
  byte-identical. SuperNova's L-R balance moves from -4.70 to -3.03 dB (ReBirth's
  whole-mix export reads -2.03), Trakka Rekka's from -1.26 to -0.99 (-0.69);
  the other changed songs with a whole-mix export barely move (`PARITY.md`).
- The byte-neutral hash holds (its `--no-fx` solos zero every send), and
  `--no-fx` whole songs are byte-identical before and after.
- `reborn_roundtrip` is 16 round-tripped, 0 problems, 0 skipped, and re-saved
  songs render identically to the originals, full rack and `--no-fx`, 90 s.
- An unedited open, play and save is still byte-identical to a plain save on all
  16 songs and both 303 voices (F1), and a delay edit moves exactly the DELY
  chunk's byte.
- `test_delay_plays_its_track` covers the start state, a timed ride landing at
  its tick, `delayOn` round-tripping through track param 0, and a switched-off
  unit adding nothing to the render.

What ReBirth does to the echo ring while the unit is off was unmeasured here
(no demo song switches it); the 2026-09-27 ride fixtures measured it, and the
ride timing, below. What happens to echoes in flight when Steps or the grid is
ridden is still unmeasured. Full record in `PARITY.md` and `FINDINGS_AUDIT.md`.

### R5. The loader dropped the DELY and COMP tracks' timed rides

The loader read only the tick-0 dumps of the DELY and COMP tracks and
discarded every later event (BACKLOG entry 5). The engine did not play them,
and a save could not write back what the loader never kept, so the save-path
fix needed them. **Fixed for the save path** in 9bdbf4a: every DELY event and
the COMP track's timed rides are carried as `AutoTarget::Delay` and
`AutoTarget::Comp`, and `reborn_roundtrip` finds them back event for event on
all 16 demo songs. The delay's rides **play** since E3 was fixed (57c7cfb).
The compressor's rides **play** since 2026-10-01: ReBirth plays them
(`fixtures-2026-10-01/comp-rides`, each ride within 0.03 dB of its end
setting), and BACKLOG entry 5 is closed. The same probe fixed the compressor's
engine integration; see `PARITY.md`, "The compressor's rides and topology".

### G1. The compressor cannot be switched on from the GUI

Section processing requires `compressorOn` (`engine.cpp:839`, `:863`). Only
a loaded song ever sets it (`engine.cpp:198`); neither editor does. The C
button in the dock mixer strip (`app/src/dock/MixerStrip.h:50-53`) and the
classic editor's equivalent route the compressor but leave it off. On the demo
song or any song that did not enable it, the button does nothing audible. The
compressor's default ratio is also 0, which is an exact bypass, so even when on it
needs its Ratio raised.

Related, and a control-completeness gap against the manual: the PCF and
distortion units each have their own on/off (PCF track param 0, DIST track
param 0; manual p62-63). The GUI never exposes them. It sets them as a side
effect of routing (`MixerStrip.h:44`, `:48`; `PluginEditor.cpp:623`, `:632`),
and the section LEDs show routing rather than whether the effect is running.
A song that routes the distortion with the unit off shows a lit D and no
distortion.

**Fix:** expose the three unit switches as LED toggles on their effect blocks,
and have the section LEDs reflect routing AND unit state. Byte-neutral.

### RT1. `getenv` on the audio thread

`BassSynth303::process` builds about 75 probe constants per call as
`const X = []{ getenv... }()` (`src/devices/bass_synth.cpp:392` onward, for
example `:414`, `:424`, `:513`, `:532`, `:590`, `:658`, `:680`, `:801`, `:900`,
`:1151`, `:1189`). They are locals, not `static`, so they re-read the
environment on **every** call, which is every slice of every block for each
TPT voice. `setStepTiming` does it once per step (`:226`). The default voice
does it once per note (`bass_open303.cpp:151`).

MSVC's `getenv` takes the CRT environment lock and scans the environment
block. That is a lock plus a linear string search on the audio thread,
thousands of times a second. Nothing in the repo sets the environment in-process
(checked), so reading once is safe.

**Fix:** make them `static const`, as the other probes in the same function
already are. Byte-neutral; the pooled hash must hold. Render time before and
after will quantify the saving.

### RT2. Heap allocation on the audio thread

`Processor::setParamByName` finds a parameter by building every `ParamInfo`
in turn (`include/reborn/processor.h:44-54`). Each one constructs three
`std::string`s, and `Drum909::paramInfo` concatenates for its eleven levels
(`src/devices/drum909.cpp:298-300`). Names like `level_Closed Hat` exceed
MSVC's 15-character small-string buffer, so these allocate.

The engine calls this on the audio thread in two places:
- **Every 909 step,** to set `accent_cell` (`engine.cpp:738`), about 30
  ParamInfos per step.
- **Every knob automation event** (`engine.cpp:462-590`).

Songs ride knobs by the thousand (G-Spot has 6022 events).

**Fix:**
- Resolve the parameter indices once, at construction or `loadSong`, and call `setParam(index, ...)`.
- Give `accent_cell` a direct setter on the drum device.

Byte-neutral.

### G2. Live SOLO freezes the other voices

The mixer strip's S button (`MixerStrip.h:58-61`) and the classic editor
(`PluginEditor.cpp:598`) drive `Engine::soloMask`. The header documents it as
a debug and analysis solo (`include/reborn/engine.h:117-120`), and it skips
calling `process()` on the other instruments (`engine.cpp:808`). Their
envelopes, sample positions and the 303's gate countdown stop. On un-solo, a
crash cymbal resumes mid-tail and a gated 303 note can hang.

**Fix:** a separate live-solo mask applied as gain (process, then zero), so
voices keep running. Leave `soloMask` for the render tools. Byte-neutral for
renders.

## Parity, measure before changing

### P1. 909 flam lands at the wrong time on a shuffled step

`flamAt_` is set to the flam gap measured from the **start of the step**
(`engine.cpp:743-745`). A shuffled 909 pattern fires its odd steps late
(`pendingAt_`, `engine.cpp:768-769`). When that delayed trigger schedules the
flam, the gap is still counted from the step start. So the second hit lands
at max(gap, delay) instead of delay + gap, and fires almost at once when the
gap is shorter than the swing delay.

Fourteen of the sixteen demo songs have flam cells, and shuffle is per-pattern.
**Measure:** a 909 fixture with flam cells on odd steps of a shuffled pattern,
at two flam amounts and two shuffle amounts. **Likely fix:**
`flamAt_ = sampleInStep_ + gap`.

### P2. Patterns shorter than 16 steps restart every bar

The sequencer's `step_` wraps at 16 (`engine.cpp:338`). Devices index
`step_ % length` (`engine.cpp:632`, `:675`), so a 12-step pattern plays steps
0 to 11, then 0 to 3, then restarts at the bar line. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:57`
says devices index `globalStep % patternLength`, which is polymeter. The manual
says each section "loops independently" (p147, per the `rebirth-manual`
skill). ReBirth's behaviour has not been measured, and the code and the doc
disagree. **Measure:** a 303 at length 12 against a 909 at length 16, eight
bars, onset positions.

### P3. The sample-mode snare has a second, synthesised noise layer (measured)

`loadSampleSetFrom` bakes the real noise recording into each snare sample.
It then mutes the drum machines' synthesised snare noise by setting `sd_snap`
to 0 (`src/io/sampleset.cpp:142`, `:185`). But every shipped song and every
rbsgen fixture carries a tick-0 `sd_snap` automation event on both machines
(checked across all 16 demo songs and the fixtures). At `play()` that event
re-arms the synthesised layer (`drum909.cpp:486`, `drum808.cpp:424`), so
sample mode plays both layers.

Measured on the 909 snappy fixtures against ReBirth, removing the synthesised
layer by patching the tick-0 event:

| Snappy | As shipped | Layer removed |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | level -0.20 dB, brightness -0.02 | -0.20, -0.02 (no change) |
| 64 | -0.23, -0.37 | -0.26, -0.43 |
| 127 | -0.20, -0.84 | **-0.85, -1.34** |

The snare mix law was fitted with the extra layer present and has absorbed
it. Removing it alone makes parity worse. It also means a mid-song snappy ride
moves only the synthesised layer, never the recording. **Recommendation:** do
not "fix" the ordering by itself. If the snare is revisited, remove the
layer and refit `gsnap` in the same change, verified against the sds, sdo and
sdx sets.

## Low-medium

### S1. Parsers recurse without a depth limit

`findLeaves` (`src/io/rbs.cpp:66-81`), `findInfo` (`src/io/rbm.cpp:154`) and
both `.rbm` walkers (`rbm.cpp:263`, `:320`) recurse on nested
`CAT`/`FORM`/`LIST`/`PROP` chunks with no depth limit. Each level costs 12
bytes of file, so a crafted file of about 1 MB nests roughly 87,000 deep and
overflows the stack. The app crashes when the file is opened, or when a
DAW restores a session. **Fix:** a depth cap of 16 (real files nest 2 or 3
deep), plus a case in `test_parsers_reject_hostile_input`.

## Low

- **L1. `loadSong` leaks state from the previous song.**
  - A channel is only ever muted by `channelOn` and never unmuted (`engine.cpp:210`).
  - Mixer and effect state is kept wholesale when the new song lacks the chunk.
  - In the app, a channel muted by song A's automation stays muted in song B unless B's tick-0 dump unmutes it.
  - **Fix:** reset `mix_` and the unit and target state to defaults at the top of `loadSong`. Byte-neutral (renders use a fresh engine).
- **L2. `saveSongFile` holds the engine lock across the whole disk write** (`PluginProcessor.cpp:150`), so playback drops out for the duration. `saveSessionFile` already does it right: capture under the lock, write outside it. Separately, `getStateInformation` calls a hosted plugin's `getStateInformation` under the lock (`:262-268`, via `captureSession`), and DAWs autosave during playback.
  - The `saveSongFile` half was fixed with W3 (80ec53a). The hosted-plugin half is unchanged.
- **L3. Sample-path voices are not reset.** `Drum909::reset` and `Drum808::reset` clear only the synthesis voices (`drum909.cpp:291`, `drum808.cpp:223`). Sample voices (`smpOn_`, snare noise, BD lowpass) keep ringing through `play()` and `prepare()`.
- **L4. Compressor units in the GUI are fictional.** `paramInfo` labels 0..1 byte values as ":1" and "dB" (`compressor.cpp:202-203`), so the dials read "0.5 :1" and "1.0 dB". Either show 0..100 like every other control, or show the measured threshold in dBFS via `thresholdDbOfByte`.
- **L5. Dead compressor setup in the engine.** The constructor sets `makeup`, which no longer exists, and its comment still describes the retired textbook compressor (`engine.cpp:52-58`). `EffectInit`'s comment claims its compressor defaults "mirror the DSP defaults" (0.5 and 0.6 against the DSP's 0.0 and 1.0), and says ratio and threshold are "still undecoded" (`pattern.h:315-333`). They were decoded and signed off on 2026-09-17.
- **L6. The PCF pattern column in the arrangement is dead.** `SongStep::pcfPattern`, `Engine::pcfPat_`, `selectPcfPattern` and `activePcfPattern` never reach the PCF unit, which reads its own `pattern` parameter (`engine.cpp:274-276`, `:356`; `engine.h:59`). ReBirth's arrangement has no PCF column: the pattern is a knob ridden on the PCF track. `activePcfPattern()` reports a stale value once the GUI stepper is used. Remove the field, or derive it from the unit's parameter.
- **L7. The LEVEL dial runs past ReBirth's maximum.** The dock's LEVEL dial runs 0 to 3.2 (`MixerStrip.h:31`), while byte 127 is `faderGain(127, kChannelUnityGain)` = 2.49, so the top 2.2 dB is out of range. It is also continuous where the file stores bytes. Step it in fader bytes so saves are exact (pairs with W2).
- **L8. The delay line holds 4 s** (`delay.cpp:49`). 16 triplet steps clamp below 80 BPM, and 16 straight steps below 60 BPM. Unmeasured whether ReBirth also clamps; a 16 s ring costs 2.8 MB.
- **L9. `PCF::gainFor` runs every sample** (`pcf.cpp:579`): a table walk and a `pow` on a value that is constant across the block. Hoist it. Byte-neutral.
- **L10. The classic editor writes mixer fields without the lock** (`PluginEditor.cpp:568-588`), where the dock strip locks. These are benign races on floats and bools, but inconsistent.

## Open decisions

Two design questions from the save-path work, put to Luke:

1. **Song-mode edits reach only the panel.** The GUI's edits go into the
   chunks, and the tracks keep the recorded start. So after a song's first
   save and reopen, Song mode plays the recorded start, not the edit. That
   is ReBirth-faithful: ReBirth's own route from the panel into the song is
   "Copy touched controls to Loop/Song", or "Initialize Loop from Pattern
   Mode" (both p83).
2. **A shortened arrangement cannot be saved shorter.** The loader sizes the
   arrangement from the last event on the instrument and mixer tracks, and
   the writer keeps every automation event, so automation past a new, earlier
   end restores the old length on reload.
