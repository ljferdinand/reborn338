## The last thing found, and it is not finished

Luke's ear on a flam test song caught that ReBirth's KICK sounded doubled where ours
sounded like nothing was applied, though the kick carried no flam marker. That
unravelled into a real misreading of the 909 pattern format:

- **ACCENT is column 0 on BOTH machines**, exactly as on the 808.
- The 909's **cell value is a separate axis: 1 soft, 2 normal, 3 and up FLAM.**
- The earlier reading, 3 as accent and 4 as flam, was wrong in both halves.

Consequences already fixed: the accent law was re-measured with clean fixtures and
is `1 + 3.967 * knob` (the old `0.773 + 3.070` was contaminated by flam, which is
why it absurdly had an accented hit quieter than a normal one at knob 0); the 808's
independently measured depth is 3.95, so **both machines share one accent law**.
Soft cells now play at 0.40 and flam cells at 0.637 per hit, both within 0.03 dB of
ReBirth on isolated fixtures.

**This mattered across the shipped set: 14 of the 16 demo songs carry soft cells
(2423) and flam cells (281) on their 909 parts**, which we were playing at full
velocity and as accents respectively.

## OPEN, and the most important thread to pick up

Rendering Trakka Rekka's 909 against the one existing 909-solo export
(`renders/02b-Trakka-Rekka-RB338-909.wav`) shows the fix moving every band toward
ReBirth (envelope correlation 0.544 -> 0.743) but leaving big residuals: we are
2.2 dB quieter overall and carry far more 300-1200 Hz (11.2% against 2.7%).

**Luke's ear says the two sound drastically different, with a loud snare at the
opening of ours that ReBirth does not have. Do not chase that against this export
without re-exporting first.** Evidence the export is not a valid reference:

- It contains **11,453 clipped samples** (0.121%, peak at full scale). A solo-909
  export with the effect rack off should not clip, which points at the master
  compressor having been on, or the effect rack live. The ab-parity protocol
  requires the compressor verified off, and this file predates that discipline (it
  sits in the `renders/` root under the old `02b-` naming, not in a dated batch).
- Our render is roughly 5x quieter than it for the first 69 seconds, then peaks.
  A structural difference that large is more likely a different export condition
  than a voice-level error.

**Next step: re-export Trakka Rekka's 909 with `rbdrive`, effect rack off and the
master compressor verified off**, then re-measure. Only after that is a residual
worth chasing. If the fresh export still shows the opening difference, the next
suspects are the arrangement decode (which pattern rows we select) and the drum
track automation mute, whose polarity is inverted (see `CLAUDE.md`).

## Everything else open on the 909

- Cell value 4 is a louder flam variant we decode but never emit; ours reads 2.7 dB
  under ReBirth's. No shipped song uses it. Accepted residual.
- SD tone is latched into the pre-mix at load, so it cannot vary per hit (same as
  the 808).
- The closed hat's mid-knob decay runs about 3 ms long.
- `909_bd0`, a 1.918 s very low kick recording, is never loaded and its role is
  unexplained. It is NOT the long-decay kick (measured).
- Only Trakka Rekka has a 909-solo export. G-Spot would be the next worth having:
  it has the most flam cells of any song (86).

## Elsewhere, from the control-inventory audit

`docs/research/CONTROL_INVENTORY_AUDIT.md` has the full record. Still unfixed, and each
belongs to its own future supertask rather than a patch now:

- The delay never decodes triplet mode (DELY byte 2) or pan (byte 4), both named
  in our own comment one line above the parser that skips them.
- The compressor never decodes ratio or threshold at all (the engine hard-sets
  bypass), and exposes attack, release and makeup, which ReBirth has no controls
  for.
- The `PCF ` chunk is never parsed, so all six PCF controls are dropped.
