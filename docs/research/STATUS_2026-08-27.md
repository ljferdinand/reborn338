## What this week actually changed

It began as "re-export Trakka Rekka's 909 with the compressor off" and turned into
nine defects, each found by chasing the one before it. Full detail is in
`PARITY.md` (the measured laws), `DRUM909_FINDINGS.md` (the synthesis pass) and
`FINDINGS_AUDIT.md` (what was retired and why). In short:

**The file format.** Drum knob automation was discarded at decode, so we loaded
the save-time chunk header instead of the song-start dump. The 909 param table had
been built from the manual's MIDI map rather than the file's own byte order. And
the accent column is a VALUE, not a flag: the 808 writes 1 and the 909 writes 2,
so every 909 accent in every song had been playing at half strength.

**The mixer, none of it previously measured.** Both faders are exponential at
about 0.370 dB per byte; pan is not equal-power. Clodhopper had been 8 dB too loud.

**Three saturators ReBirth does not have**, including a compressor that ran on
every render regardless of what the song said. That last one means any loud
measurement this project made before 2026-08-26 was compressed on our side only.

**The 909 synthesis path**, never fitted against isolated exports before this.

## Two process notes worth carrying forward

**A control whose fixture value never moves has not been measured**, however many
fixtures use it. Three mixer laws hid for months because rbsgen writes 100 into
both faders and 64 into pan, which is exactly where each law is anchored.

**A saturator is a parity surface.** If we have a nonlinearity and cannot point at
the measurement that put it there, it is a bug. Three were found in one day and
all three were wrong.

And one about this document's own genre: `STATUS_2026-08-25.md` left the Trakka
Rekka question open with a confident theory about the export being at fault. The
theory was wrong; the fault was our decoder. A status doc should say what was
measured and what is open, and be sparing with what it thinks the answer will be.
