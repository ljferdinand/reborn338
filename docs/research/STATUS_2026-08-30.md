## The one decision that blocked the next move: SETTLED 2026-08-31, no

The 102 Hz highpass is NOT the fix. Scored on the net's own metric on the
listening songs rather than the static fixtures, `OSC_HP` 102 moves the full
band shape away from ReBirth on every song, saw ones included (groove saw +0.75,
tour saw +0.61 dB rms). It improves the brightness RATIO only by cutting the low
denominator while the 23-band SHAPE worsens, and it over-brightens the square
which was already right. The net was right and the waveform's INTERPRETATION was
wrong: our saw's darkness is missing HIGHS, not an excess of lows, so cutting the
lows is the wrong direction. Full writeup in `BASS303_FINDINGS.md` section 15.
The open 303 waveform step is now a source-level high-harmonic probe, a fresh
investigation. Nothing shipped; `OSC_HP`, `A_HP`, `DRIVE` stay at defaults.

## The original blocking decision, for the record

**Does ReBirth's 303 have a one-pole highpass at about 102 Hz, before the
filter?** Two sound measurements disagree and I could not break the tie.

**The waveform says yes.** Pitch-synchronously averaged over twelve cycles,
ReBirth's saw is not a ramp: it is a sharp step decaying exponentially to a
plateau inside one cycle, which is what a ramp looks like after a highpass and is
not what a lowpass does to anything. The settle time constant across six pitches
spanning an octave is 1.59, 1.58, 1.57, 1.56, 1.54, 1.52 ms, **constant to 4
percent**. A fixed time constant under changing pitch is a fixed-FREQUENCY filter
and cannot be a property of a waveform. Ours is a constant 0.29 of a CYCLE
whatever the pitch, which is a waveform property and not a filter. Adding
`OSC_HP` 102 takes the waveform-shape error from 0.163 to 0.054.

**The regression net says no**, at +0.815 over 373 cells with 361 worse and not
one of the sixteen sets improving.

**Two ways to settle it, and they are not equivalent.**

1. `listen-303-hp-2026-08-30/` is cut and waiting: ReBirth, our shipped build and
   our highpassed build on the saw songs. If ReBirth sounds THINNER than both,
   the highpass is right and the net is measuring something else. If ReBirth
   sounds FULLER than the highpassed version, the highpass is wrong however good
   the waveform match looks.
2. Build a PHASE-AWARE regression net. Slower, and the better long-term answer,
   because the project currently cannot see this class of difference anywhere.

**Do not ship the highpass on the shape metric alone.** It is one day old, it was
built to answer this one question, and 361 of 373 cells worse deserves better.

**2. The resonance-0 hole in `LF_HP`.** The law is
`fc(r) = LF_HP_MAX * r / (r + LF_HP_KNEE)`, exactly zero at resonance 0.
`bass_synth.cpp` has said `KNOWN INCOMPLETE AT RESONANCE 0` since 2026-08-02.
It has now been rediscovered THREE separate times wearing different hats: as the
level tilt in section 6, as the passband droop in section 6a, and as the
"oscillator gap" in sections 13 and 14. B8 pins the shape: the open cells at
resonance 0 want more highpass and the engaged cells at resonance 16 want
slightly less, which is a corner that DECREASES with resonance where the shipped
law increases. That contradicts the 2026-08-02 fit, so it is structural work and
not a tweak. It is the largest known-open item in the 303.

## The learnings, which are the real output

Ten rules earned this week, each from a specific failure. The first four are new
today and are already in the `ab-parity` skill.

**A no-op must not look like a result.** A clean `+0.000` is what a change the net
cannot see looks like, AND what a setting that never reached the renderer looks
like. Three instances: `REBORN_BASS_ENGINE` leaving every 303 constant unread
because the default voice is Open303, a `MIXR` chunk patch the track overrode,
and a shell quoting slip that sent identical overrides to both sides.
`regress_ab.py` now refuses identical settings and checks whether the rendered
pairs are BIT-IDENTICAL, because checking the audio is the only version that
catches all three.

**Every metric here throws phase away.** Harmonic magnitudes, third-octave band
energy, brightness ratios. Two signals with identical magnitude spectra can be
entirely different waveforms, and that is not hypothetical: a magnitude metric
reported our saw matching ReBirth's "to a fifth of a dB" on renders whose cycles
do not overlay. **The project has no phase-aware instrument at all.**

**A defect hides in what every fixture HOLDS CONSTANT, not in what they vary.**
Three times this week. Every 303 fixture pins env mod at 0, so a level law keyed
off the cutoff knob could not be told from one keyed off the corner until a demo
song at env mod 78 exposed it in a minute. Every 303 fixture runs the SAW, so a
waveform-specific difference was invisible. Every accent fixture was either all
isolated notes or all continuous sixteenths, so no fit could be scored on both.

**When two instruments disagree, they are covering different regimes.** Four for
four this week. It has never once been that one instrument was broken.

The six standing ones, restated because each cost a round:

**A chunk is the save-time panel; the track is what plays.** Four instances now:
the PCF's routing, the 303's knobs, a `MIXR` channel mute and the per-section
distortion switches.

**An export you did not make is not a reference until you check it.** Exports are
cheap and unattended: make the reference yourself, or cross-check the inherited
one against something you did make.

**A denominator differs from its numerator in exactly one thing**, and a filter
you cannot switch off is not one.

**A constant that was absorbing someone else's error MOVES when that error is
fixed.** If it does not move, suspect the fix. The accent time constants moved
when the squelch got a law instead of a constant, and the single-point score came
out better than shipped rather than merely recovered.

**Draw it.** Two numbers per cell cannot say which of two measurements is
confused. matplotlib was installed the whole time and unused; plotting 45 cells
answered a sign disagreement in ten seconds.

**A window that straddles the next event is not part of this one.**
