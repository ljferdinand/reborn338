## The diode fork is CLOSED, and the sequencing question it raised is answered

Luke asked whether the muted chirp and the resonance-0 low-end mean we must go back
to the filter CORE (the coupled diode ladder) before tweaking further. Measured and
answered 2026-09-03 (`BASS303_FINDINGS` 24, 25): NO.

- The dense comb the diode design was built to reproduce is disproven. ReBirth's
  high-resonance spectrum is peaked, not a comb (flatness ~0, no discrete
  inter-harmonic peaks), on static and driven fixtures exported for this.
- ReBirth's one real extra, 10 to 40 dB more inter-harmonic energy, is ALIASING.
  Lowering our oversampling reproduces then overshoots it; heavy drive is inert as
  the physics requires; the diode core does not tune it. So it is an oversampling
  CHARACTER, not the coupled topology.

So all three high-resonance differences, the comb, the prominence, and the
richness, have non-diode explanations. The current buffered-TPT core can reach
ReBirth. The corner and resonance tuning are safe from a core swap because there is
no core swap coming. The diode fork stays as a documented historical option.

## Remaining 303 items, none blocking, on the current core

- **Reso 90 to 110 prominence**, 3 to 4 dB short, bounded by the k=4.0
  self-oscillation cap. Accepted residual after the partial `K_B_SLOPE`.
- **The high-resonance grit / chirp (FLAGGED FOR REVISIT).** Luke heard tour-ALLUP
  as less chirpy than ReBirth across the track. Traced 2026-09-03 to the
  RESONANCE-TRANSIENT aliasing (section 24): the sharp per-cycle feedback spike that
  folds into the audible band at low oversampling. It is coupled to the transient
  sharpness through the same `TPT_OS` knob (1x aliases into grit but rounds the
  transient, 4x is sharp but clean), and ReBirth has both at once. A blunt OS cut
  fails the net (folds low notes) and a naive-oscillator source did nothing (its
  aliasing is above the filter corner). Getting grit AND transient together is a
  real buffered-ladder limitation, and it is the ONE narrow, character-only reason
  the coupled diode ladder could still matter, distinct from the disproven comb.
  Left as an accepted residual now. **REVISIT TRIGGER: if 303 parity, or whole-mix
  parity as later components land, is not showing substantive improvement and this
  high-res character is a suspected reason, reopen it, and the coupled diode ladder
  is then on the table for the grit specifically.** Not worth a structural rewrite
  for a subtle hash on its own.
- **The accent brightness ramp** (`ACC_SWEEP`), the accent's own chirp about 0.2 to
  0.3 octaves short at peak accent. Belongs to the accent step.
- **The reso-88 static top character**, our groove runs +3.7 dB at 7 kHz against
  ReBirth even before the corner fix, invariant to ceiling and Q-shed. Own item.
- **~~The resonance-0 `LF_HP` hole~~ CLOSED, re-measured 2026-09-03 (section 25).**
  It was reinterpreted in section 6 as a LEVEL tilt and the shipped `LVL_CUT` law
  fixed it: at reso 0 ours now gains 10.6 dB across the cutoff knob against ReBirth's
  10.7, a ~1 dB gap where section 6 measured 8.4. Section 14's "largest open
  structural item" framing was stale.
- **The HIGH-resonance level tilt (the real open level item).** At reso 127 ReBirth
  GAINS ~2 dB across the cutoff knob while ours DROOPS ~3.5 dB, a 5.7 dB gap;
  smaller wiggles at reso 32 (ours ~1 dB low) and 64 (ours ~1 to 2 dB high). A level
  tilt, invisible to the band-shape net. Pre-existing (K_B_SLOPE added ~0.3 dB). The
  fix is a high-reso cutoff-dependent level term, the mirror of reso-0's `LVL_CUT`,
  or a makeup that grows with cutoff at high k.
